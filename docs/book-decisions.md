# The book's decisions that the labs cite

The labs cite a few of the book's own decisions by number, from D-010 to D-040. The book's decision log isn't
published; these are the parts of it the labs depend on, in short.

- **D-010, the diagram style (28 October 2026).** One restrained palette that stays legible in gray, and official provider icons only in the three platform chapters. W5's cost fake (`workflows/w5-drift/servers/cost_fake.py`) cites D-010 for the rule that the book prints no absolute prices; that rule comes from the book's style guide, not from D-010. The comment stays as it is, because the red-team results of chapter 15 record the file's hash.
- **D-018, the cloud lab (28 October 2026).** The labs run locally first. Cloud work happens in trial accounts the author created, under a cap of 150 USD a month per cloud; the billing alarms in `terraform/billing-alarms/` are the first Terraform applied in each, and every lab is destroyed at the end of the session that applied it. Local models run on the author's workstation, on an RTX 5080 with 16 GB of memory.
- **D-029, the AWS free plan as the cap (29 October 2026).** The AWS lab account is on AWS's free plan, which charges nothing beyond its credits, so the credits are the cap and an apply no longer waits for a budget alert's email. The rest of D-018 stands: the budget goes first, every lab is destroyed at the end of the session that applied it, and the plan's state is read again before each apply. Services the free plan doesn't offer are found at the first apply and recorded.
- **D-032, Claude Haiku 5.5 in Canada as a candidate (29 October 2026).** On 22 October 2026 AWS added a Canadian geographic profile for Claude Haiku 5.5, served from ca-central-1 and ca-west-1. Larkspur keeps Amazon Nova Lite behind `larkspur-chat` and treats Haiku 5.5 as a candidate: the alias moves only when a workflow's golden set passes on it.
- **D-033, W1 against a managed model (29 October 2026).** W1's twenty-five cases ran with `larkspur-chat` on Nova Lite through the Canada profile, with the Restricted alias and the embeddings left on the workstation (`workflows/w1-knowledge-assistant/eval/results/2026-10-29/`). Nova Lite passed 17, against gpt-oss-20b's 24. Haiku 5.5's Canada profile answered the lab account "not available for this account", so it is unmeasured.
- **D-034, the AWS account's one Region (29 October 2026).** The lab account came from AWS's newer sign-up, whose service control policy denies every action outside the account's assigned Region, us-east-2, and the global services' Regions, except Bedrock's model calls; the account can't change it. Model calls through the Canada profile still run from ca-central-1. A check whose answer doesn't depend on the Region runs in us-east-2 and says so.
- **D-035, the Azure lab identity and its cap (29 October 2026).** The Azure lab runs in a Free Trial subscription with its spending limit on, and that limit is the cap, as the credits are on AWS. Chapter 8's Terraform runs as an app registration with Contributor on the subscription and the right to assign and remove only the two roles the Terraform grants (Container Registry Repository Reader and Cognitive Services OpenAI User). The budget goes first, each lab is destroyed at the end of the session that applied it, and the subscription's state is read before each apply.
- **D-036, chapter 6's lab where the account allows (30 October 2026).** The lab's `region` stays ca-central-1 for Larkspur's deployment and takes another Region only with `synthetic_data = true`; the guardrail profile follows the Region's geography, and the budget's email is optional. `labs/ch06/sandbox` makes the stand-ins the lab takes from Larkspur's platform. The lab was applied in us-east-2 on 30 October 2026 and destroyed in the same session. The book keeps Larkspur in Canada and says the lab ran in us-east-2 on synthetic data.
- **D-037, chapter 8's lab applied once in the trial (2 November 2026).** The lab ran once in the Azure trial, with `labs/ch08/sandbox` standing in for Larkspur's network and the budget applied first. Fourteen of twenty resources came up. What the trial refused (the Standard deployment of gpt-4o, for want of quota, and ACR Tasks) and what the lab's identity had no right to do (write two policy definitions) are recorded in `labs/ch08/results/2026-11-02/` rather than worked around. Everything was destroyed in the same session.
- **D-039, the Azure trial stays a trial (2 November 2026).** The subscription isn't upgraded. The two facts that need a deployment and an image (whether a hosted agent deployed with the SDK keeps an endpoint reachable from outside the network, and whether the project's user-assigned identity pulls the image) are checked against Microsoft's pages instead of a run, and chapter 8 labels them "per the documentation, not a run". The rule holds for every lab: a fact the account can't reach is written as the documentation's, never as the lab's.
- **D-040, Google Cloud through Workload Identity Federation (2 November 2026).** The trial project's organization policies forbid creating or uploading service account keys, so the lab holds none. It authenticates with a self-signed X.509 client certificate through a workload identity pool and an X.509 provider, so there is no key to rotate and the certificate's validity is the limit. The spending cap is the Free Trial's credits.
