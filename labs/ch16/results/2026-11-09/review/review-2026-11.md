# Monthly review: November 2026

Prepared on 9 November 2026 for Ingrid Holm's monthly review (O-D15), from the files chapter 16's lab kept, with no model and no cloud call. The prices are the dated table's (`labs/ch16/ledger/prices.yaml`: 49 entries, each with its source and its date, 28 October to 3 November 2026), and every share of a budget is of the manifest's `per_run_usd` or `per_month_usd`. Sections 1 to 5 and 7 read files that carry no month of their own (the kept runs, the estimated volumes, step 6's simulated month, the register on the review's date), and sections 6 and 8 read the records dated in November 2026. Every table says what its figures are, in five words:

- measured: read or computed from a kept run, a drill, or a live run on this workstation or in the lab account.
- priced: measured tokens or calls times a price of the dated table, at chapter 6's design: what the same tokens would cost on the managed alias, since the local models cost nothing.
- estimated: Larkspur's monthly volumes (ledger/volumes.yaml, each with its source) and anything multiplied by them, and the guardrail's estimate, which no cost line carries.
- simulated: step 6's month, 30 days built from the kept runs with five injected events, marked simulated wherever it appears.
- recorded: a date, a state, or a change record read from a register or a record file, not measured by the lab.
