# Open source licensing

**Effective:** 2025-06-05 · **Approved by:** CIO · **Review cycle:** yearly

## Purpose

Keeps Larkspur's use of open source within licence terms.

## Rules

1. Permissive licences (MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0) are allowed.
2. Weak copyleft (LGPL, MPL-2.0) is allowed for unmodified use as a library.
3. Strong copyleft (GPL, AGPL) and source-available licences need Legal approval.
4. The SBOM generated in CI is the record; the licence check fails the build on a denied licence.

## Exceptions

Exceptions are requested in ServiceNow (catalogue item **Policy exception**) and approved by legal-regulatory for up to 180 days.
