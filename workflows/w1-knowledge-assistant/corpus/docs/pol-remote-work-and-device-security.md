# Remote work and device security

**Effective:** 2025-01-24 · **Approved by:** Chief Risk Officer · **Review cycle:** yearly

## Purpose

Rules for working from outside Larkspur offices.

## Rules

1. Use Larkspur-managed devices enrolled in Intune for all work.
2. Connect through the Larkspur VPN or Zero Trust client for internal systems.
3. Do not print Confidential or Restricted documents at home.
4. Report lost devices to the Service Desk within 1 hour.

## Exceptions

Exceptions are requested in ServiceNow (catalogue item **Policy exception**) and approved by information-security for up to 90 days.
