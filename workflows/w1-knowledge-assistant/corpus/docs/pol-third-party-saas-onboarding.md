# Third-party SaaS onboarding

**Effective:** 2025-02-15 · **Approved by:** General Counsel · **Review cycle:** yearly

## Purpose

Every new SaaS vendor is assessed before data is shared with it.

## Rules

1. Request through the ServiceNow Vendor Risk catalogue item before signing anything.
2. Vendors receiving Confidential data need a SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent and SSO through Entra ID.
3. Restricted data may not be shared with SaaS vendors without CISO approval.
4. Vendors are re-assessed every year or after a reported breach.

## Exceptions

Exceptions are requested in ServiceNow (catalogue item **Policy exception**) and approved by procurement-and-vendor-risk for up to 180 days.
