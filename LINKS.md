# Links

The pages behind the product facts of *Agentic Workflows in the Large Enterprise*, chapter by chapter.

Before each chapter was written, its facts were collected in a fact sheet: notes that quote each page, with its address and the date it was read. The fact sheets stay with the book's working files, and their addresses are here, under each sheet's own headings. When a lab cites "the fact sheet, section 8.8" or "LINKS.md, chapter 16, section 8.8", the pages are under heading 8.8 of that chapter below. Where a sheet names its pages by a key, such as S35 or W11, the key stands before the address. The book's Appendix A names its sources by page title, and the last section lists those titles with their addresses.

The pages were read for the book between 28 October and 9 November 2026, and again on 10 November 2026 for the last check of its product facts. A vendor may move or rewrite a page at any time; the page's title and the date are the way to find it again.
